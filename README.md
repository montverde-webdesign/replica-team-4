# Replica Challenge

Your team rebuilds a website from screenshots only: same content, same look. Your team's site will be live at
`https://montverde-webdesign.github.io/<your-team-repo>/`

## Your brief

Open `briefs/` and then your level's folder:

- `mockup-desktop.png` and `mockup-phone.png`: what your site must look like
- `parts-map.png`: who builds which part
- `style-card.md`: fonts, colors, sizes, and CSS hints
- `content.md`: all the text, so you don't have to retype it from the picture
- `images/`: copy these into the main `images` folder

## Team rules

1. **Sync before you start. Sync when you stop.** Click **Sync Changes** (or the circle arrows at the bottom of VS Code) at the start of class and every time you commit.
2. **Stay in your lane.** Type only between your own PART lines in `index.html` and in your own CSS file.
3. **Need something in `base.css`?** Ask the Part A owner. Only they edit it.
4. **Commit small and often.** Every time one thing works, commit with a message that starts with a verb, like `Add menu items` or `Style game cards`.
5. **Help by talking, not typing.** You can point, explain, and look things up for a teammate, but they keep the keyboard.
6. **See the words "merge conflict"?** Stop and call your teacher. Don't click anything yet.
